KBin Checker Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Summary

KBin Checker has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It does not ask for, store or transmit full card numbers, expiry dates, CVV codes or cardholder names.

What the app sends

When you tap Check BIN, the 6 to 8 digits you entered are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our lookup service, which is hosted on Cloudflare and retrieves the public issuer record from our BIN data provider, HandyAPI (handyapi.com). As with any internet request, these services can see your IP address. The request contains no name, device identifier or other personal information, and results are cached by BIN only, with no link to who asked.

What stays on your device

Your lookup history, favorites and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded. You can switch history off or clear it in Settings, and uninstalling the app removes everything.

Permissions

The app uses only the Internet permission, which is required to perform lookups.

Children

KBin Checker is a general reference tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and in the app.
